Good News Network · 4 hours ago

A spacecraft called BepiColombo has successfully arrived at Mercury, only the third mission ever to reach our solar system's innermost planet. It's a reminder of how much patient, careful engineering it takes to explore even our closest planetary neighbors. No explosions, no drama, just years of hard work paying off exactly as planned.
