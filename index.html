Major Success Sees Viral Infections Fall 68% in Brazil After Releasing Wolbachia-Infected Mosquitoes

Good News Network · 2 hours ago

In Brazil, scientists released mosquitoes carrying a harmless bacteria called Wolbachia, which makes it much harder for the insects to spread viruses like dengue. After the release, children living in the treated areas had a 68% lower risk of catching these mosquito-borne diseases. The method has been proven in smaller settings before, and this trial shows it can work across a whole population. It's a gentle, natural way to protect families without relying only on sprays or chemicals.

This Blue Whale Population Grew 1,000% Since the End of Antarctic Whaling

Good News Network · 3 hours ago

Antarctic blue whales, the largest animals ever to live on Earth, have made an astonishing comeback. Their numbers have grown from just 418 whales to about 4,018 since 1973, an increase of roughly 1,000%. That works out to steady growth of about 4.5% every year. The recovery followed the end of Antarctic whaling, and it shows that when people stop harming a species and protect it, nature can bounce back.
