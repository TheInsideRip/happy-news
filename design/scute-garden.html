What one story per timeframe changes

It drops the appetite from 126 non-repeating stories a week to 21. That was the number I was most worried about — the good-news world does not produce 126 genuinely good, genuinely new stories a week, and "never repeat" made that a hard ceiling. At 21 it stops being a constraint and starts being a luxury: the same wide net now feeds a much pickier filter, so only the best thing found all morning makes it through.

It also means the whole day fits on one screen with no scrolling, and the memory file grows at roughly a quarter of the old rate.

Tell me what to adjust — turtle strength, the pink, headline size, or the plate shape — and I'll update the spec and start building.
