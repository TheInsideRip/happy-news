Housing Nonprofit Funds $16.5 Million in Eviction Relief to Assist Renters

Good News Network · just now

An Atlanta-based nonprofit has now given out $16.5 million through its Eviction Relief Fund, helping renters stay in their homes instead of losing them. Since the program began during the pandemic, it has supported more than 5,500 families who were struggling to make rent. Affordable-housing landlord Marjy Stagmeier helped build the effort to ease the pressure on tenants during hard times. It's a steady, practical kind of help that keeps real families safely housed.

High School Fills Entire Football Field With Groceries to Replenish New Jersey Food Banks

Good News Network · 2 hours ago

Students at Howell High School in New Jersey turned their football field into a sea of groceries last week, covering it end zone to end zone with bright yellow donation bags. Together they collected more than a quarter million pounds of food for local food banks during the school's second annual food drive. It's a huge show of teenage generosity, proving that when young people organize around a cause, they can make a real dent in hunger in their community. Families who rely on these food banks will feel the difference this winter.
