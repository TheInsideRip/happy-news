Live Cheetah Den Webcam Lets You Watch Mom Zura and Her 5 New Cubs

Good News Network · 1 hour ago

A cheetah named Zura gave birth to five healthy cubs earlier this month at the Smithsonian's Conservation Biology Institute in Virginia. Because cheetahs are vulnerable in the wild, every new litter born safely under expert care is a win for the species. Now anyone can tune in any time of day to watch Zura tend to her tiny cubs and even hear her gentle vocal sounds through a live webcam. It's a sweet, calming peek into new life and devoted animal motherhood. Conservationists hope these cubs will one day help support the broader cheetah population.

Star Clusters in Van Gogh's Starry Night Match the Real Taurus Sky From July 1889

Good News Network · 18 hours ago

Researchers took a close look at the swirling sky in Van Gogh's famous painting The Starry Night and found something wonderful: the stars he painted actually match the real night sky over France in July of 1889. Art historians had long guessed roughly when he painted it, but this new comparison of star clusters and the moon's position pins it down with real precision. It's a lovely reminder that Van Gogh wasn't just painting from imagination, he was capturing an actual night he witnessed. Scientists and art lovers teaming up to solve a century-old mystery is just a nice bit of human curiosity at work. It gives one of the world's most beloved paintings an even richer, truer story.
