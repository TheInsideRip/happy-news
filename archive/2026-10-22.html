Indonesia Halts 'World's Largest Deforestation Project,' Saving Potentially 4 Million Acres

Good News Network · 4 hours ago

Indonesia has pulled the plug, ahead of schedule, on what had been called the world's largest deforestation project. The massive land-clearing plan was originally meant to turn rainforest, swamps, and peatlands into farmland to boost rice production. But because the country reached its rice self-sufficiency goals three years early through other means, officials decided the destruction was no longer necessary. That decision is expected to spare roughly 4 million acres of rainforest, swamp, and peatland from being cleared. It's a rare case of a huge environmental threat being called off simply because it turned out not to be needed.

Scientists Discover a Simple, Low-Energy Way to Recycle Acrylic Glass into Brand-New Material

Good News Network · 28 hours ago

Researchers in Switzerland found a simple, low-energy way to break down acrylic glass, the stuff behind plastics like Plexiglas, back into its original building blocks. That means it can be turned into brand-new, high-quality material instead of ending up as waste. The process doesn't need harsh chemicals or huge amounts of energy, which makes it realistic for recycling plants to actually use. Acrylic glass shows up everywhere from car parts to aquariums, so this could cut down a surprising amount of plastic waste. It's a rare case where a lab discovery looks genuinely practical and affordable right away.

Samoan Island Witnesses Explosion of Native Wildlife Following Removal of Invasive Rats and Pigs

Good News Network · just now

A small Samoan island called Nu'utele has come back to life after conservationists cleared out invasive rats and pigs that had been destroying it for decades. Just one year later, the native forest is thriving again, and wildlife that had nearly vanished is bouncing back in a big way. The island is one of Samoa's most important wild places, home to rare birds and plants found almost nowhere else. It's a clear, measurable win showing how removing a harmful invader can let nature heal itself remarkably fast.
