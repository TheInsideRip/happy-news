Japan Successfully Launches Mission to Bring Home the First-Ever Martian Moon Samples

Good News Network · 1 hour ago

Japan just launched a spacecraft on its way to the two small moons that orbit Mars. The mission, called MMX, will land on one of these moons, scoop up soil samples, and bring them back to Earth for scientists to study. This would be the first time anyone has ever collected material from a Martian moon. It's a huge step for understanding how Mars and its moons formed, and the rocket lifted off successfully on October 19th.

Texas Man Finds More Than $128,000 on Top of a Bank's ATM–and Returns Every Dollar

Good News Network · 22 hours ago

A man in Texas spotted a bag stuffed with cash sitting on top of an ATM machine and, instead of keeping it, called the police right away. The bag held more than $128,000, likely left behind by a nearby business. Thanks to his honesty, every single dollar made its way back to its rightful owner. It's a simple reminder that there are still people who do the right thing even when no one is watching.
