Whale Festival Kicks Off on Amelia Island in Florida Celebrating the Return of the Right Whale

Good News Network · 3 hours ago

Every winter, endangered North Atlantic right whales swim down to the warm waters off Florida's Amelia Island to have their calves, and this week the town is throwing its annual festival to celebrate their return. It's been running since 2009, drawing families to watch for whale sightings and learn about the species. These whales are one of the rarest marine mammals on earth, so seeing them come back each year is a genuine bright spot for ocean conservation. The festival turns a conservation milestone into a joyful community event. It's a nice reminder that protecting wildlife and having fun aren't mutually exclusive.

Islands Near Portugal Turn Off Lights to Protect Seabird Fledglings Every October – 30k Already Saved

Good News Network · 14 hours ago

Every autumn on tiny Corvo island in the Azores, more than 400 residents turn off their lights for five nights in a row. It's not a power outage, it's on purpose: young seabirds get confused by artificial light and crash into buildings instead of finding their way to sea. Thanks to this simple community blackout, locals have rescued and released around 30,000 fledglings over the years. It's a beautiful example of a whole town pitching in for wildlife, no fancy technology required.
