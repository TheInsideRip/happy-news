'The forest is life': how Indigenous communities in Peru are preventing wildfires

Positive News · 14 hours ago

While wildfires have been getting worse across much of Peru, some Indigenous communities are actually seeing them decline. In the community of Paamari, people have combined traditional knowledge with active forest management to stop fires before they spread. Their methods are working so well that they're becoming a model other communities want to copy. It's a reminder that the people who know a forest best are often its best protectors. Real fires prevented, real forest saved.

A Strategy of Cash-for-Photos Turns Animal Hunters into Animal Protectors in Indonesia

Good News Network · 5 hours ago

On the island of Borneo, people who used to hunt endangered birds and orangutans are now getting paid to photograph them instead. Nine villages in Indonesia's West Kalimantan province have joined a pay-per-picture program that gives families extra income for protecting wildlife rather than harming it. The shift has turned former hunters into some of the animals' biggest defenders, since a living creature is now worth more to them than a dead one. It's a simple, clever way to align people's livelihoods with conservation, and it's already making a real difference on the ground.

Norway's Newest Marine Protected Area Shelters Rich Marine Habitat, Whales, and Seabirds

Good News Network · just now

Norway just created a huge new protected area in the ocean called Andfjorden, covering about 714 square miles of sea. That's roughly the size of 260,000 football pitches, all now safeguarded for whales, seabirds, and a wonderfully rich mix of marine life living both above and below the water. The government made this official on September 18th, so it's already law and already protecting these waters. It's a real, lasting win for one of the planet's most beautiful and biodiverse coastlines.
