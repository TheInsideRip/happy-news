Couple with Autism and ADHD Find Passion Cleaning Filthy Street Signs

Good News Network · 4 hours ago

A married couple named Paul and Ruth started a sweet new hobby together this fall: scrubbing grimy street signs back to a shine. Both have autism and ADHD, and they found that working side by side on this simple task gave them a shared sense of purpose and joy. What began as a way to spend time together has turned into a real passion, with the pair now dreaming of cleaning signs across the whole country. It's a lovely reminder that happiness can be found in the smallest, most ordinary tasks when you do them with someone you love.

Two of the 'Last Wild Ecosystems' Untouched by Humans Are Declared New Sanctuaries

Good News Network · 1 hour ago

Scientists have found a handful of remote Pacific atolls so untouched by people that they count among the last truly wild, healthy ecosystems left on Earth. These hidden islands are bursting with life, from coral to seabirds, almost exactly as they would have looked before humans arrived anywhere. Rather than just study them, officials have now officially declared two of these pristine spots protected sanctuaries. That means their reefs and wildlife get real, lasting legal shelter from fishing, development, and other human pressures. It's a rare chance to keep a piece of the planet exactly as nature made it.
