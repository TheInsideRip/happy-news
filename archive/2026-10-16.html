North America's Largest Wildlife Overpass Is Working: Animal Collisions on I-25 Down 91%

Good News Network · 4 hours ago

Colorado opened North America's biggest wildlife bridge over a busy interstate near Larkspur late last year, giving bears, elk, moose, and other animals a safe way to cross six lanes of traffic. Ten months later, the numbers are in, and they're remarkable: collisions between vehicles and animals in that stretch have dropped by 91 percent. That means fewer injured drivers, fewer heartbreaking roadside deaths for wildlife, and a model other states are already looking to copy. It's proof that a smart piece of infrastructure can quietly save lives, both human and animal, every single day.

Drape Some of This 'Plant Armor' Fabric Over a Strawberry Patch to Triple Yields with Less Pesticides

Good News Network · 2 hours ago

Scientists tested a new lightweight fabric called Plant Armor by draping it over strawberry plants in real field trials, and the results were striking. The covered plants produced more than three times as much fruit as uncovered ones, without any harm to the plants. Even better, farmers using it needed noticeably less water and far less pesticide to get that bigger harvest. It's a simple, low-tech fix that could make growing fruit cheaper, cleaner, and more reliable for farmers everywhere.

Largest Private Conservation Project in the US Hits Major Milestone as Free-Roaming Bison Pass 1,000

Good News Network · just now

Out on the Great Plains, a huge conservation ranch just hit a wonderful milestone: its free-roaming bison herd has grown past 1,000 animals. These bison live much like their wild ancestors did, grazing freely across vast grasslands that are being restored as a result. It's the largest private conservation project of its kind in the country, and this growing herd shows the land and wildlife are genuinely thriving together. A nice reminder that patient, long-term work on the land really can pay off.
