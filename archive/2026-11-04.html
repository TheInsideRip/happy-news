Brazil Expands Protected Area for 'One of the Richest and Most Sensitive Biomes on the Planet'

Good News Network · 3 hours ago

Brazil just made one of its most precious wild places even bigger and safer. Officials expanded Pantanal Matogrossense National Park by 185 square miles, growing it by more than a quarter of its original size. The Pantanal is the world's largest tropical wetland, home to jaguars, giant otters, and countless birds, and this move locks in stronger legal protection for a huge new stretch of it. It's a real, completed expansion, not a proposal, so the extra land is protected right now.

Italy Sees Collapse in its Homicide Rate to 3rd Lowest in the EU

Good News Network · 3 hours ago

Italy just posted its lowest homicide rate on record, making it the third-safest country in the EU by this measure. This is a huge turnaround from the mafia violence that once plagued parts of the country. Years of steady police work and improved security efforts are clearly paying off. For everyday Italians, it means neighborhoods that are measurably safer than they've been in decades. It's a quiet but real win worth celebrating.
