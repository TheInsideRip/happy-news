Vietnam Swiftly Protects Forests Containing the Last of the World's Most-Endangered Monkey

Good News Network · 2 hours ago

Vietnam has just set aside about 7,500 acres of stunning limestone mountains and forest to create a new conservation reserve. It's home to the Delacour's langur, one of the rarest primates on Earth, with only a small population left in the wild. This protection didn't take years of red tape -- officials moved quickly once they understood how urgent the threat was. Now these striking black-and-white monkeys, along with the wild landscape they depend on, have a real safeguard for the future. It's a rare case of conservation happening at the speed the crisis actually demanded.

18 Year-Old from the Bronx Starts Nonprofit to Sponsor School Children in Ghana

Good News Network · just now

Max Owobu, an 18-year-old high school senior from the Bronx, started his own nonprofit to help sponsor school children in Ghana. While juggling exams and college applications like any other senior, he also found time to travel to Ghana to see the work in person. His organization helps cover the costs that keep kids in the classroom. It's a heartwarming reminder of what young people can build when they decide to help others. His generosity is already changing lives on the other side of the world.
