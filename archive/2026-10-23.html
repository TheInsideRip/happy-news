One Teacher's Idea for Daily Positivity Practice Sweeps Through His Elementary School

Good News Network · 2 hours ago

A first-grade teacher in Kansas named Tyler Richards started having his students say daily affirmations like 'I am kind' and 'I can do hard things' before class begins. The simple habit caught on so well that it has now spread to other classrooms throughout his entire elementary school. Kids are heading home with more confidence and a little extra kindness in their pockets. It's a small thing that's making a real difference in how these children see themselves every day.

Vines Produce Bigger, Healthier Wine Grapes When Grown Under Solar Panels

Good News Network · just now

Scientists tried something clever: growing wine grapes underneath solar panels instead of in open fields. It turns out the vines liked the shade and protection so much that they produced bigger, healthier grapes than usual. At the same time, the panels overhead were quietly generating clean electricity. It's a neat two-for-one, more food and more renewable power from the very same patch of land. This kind of win-win farming could help both farmers and the planet going forward.
