Archaeologists Risked Their Lives to Save 30,000 Treasures from ISIS Terrorists—New Study Shows How

Good News Network · 3 hours ago

A new study reveals the remarkable story of archaeologists and museum staff who rushed to protect Syria's cultural heritage as danger closed in. Working quickly and quietly, they managed to save around 30,000 priceless artifacts from the Deir ez-Zor Museum before the area fell into the wrong hands. These objects, some thousands of years old, hold pieces of human history that could have been lost forever. Thanks to their courage and quick thinking, that history survives today for future generations to study and appreciate. It's a powerful reminder of how much people are willing to risk to protect what matters.

NYC Forces DoorDash to Pay $131 Million After Underpaying Delivery Workers

Good News Network · just now

New York City's consumer protection office just wrapped up a major enforcement case against DoorDash, and the results are already in workers' pockets. The city found the company had been shorting delivery drivers who worked hard every day to get food to people's doors. DoorDash has now paid $131.5 million total, with more than $115 million of that going straight to the workers who were owed it. It's a real win that shows city rules meant to protect gig workers actually have teeth. Thousands of delivery workers are getting money back that they rightfully earned.
