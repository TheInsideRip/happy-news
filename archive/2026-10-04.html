Rare 1-in-100M 'Ghost' Lobster Finds a New Home at University Marine Center

Good News Network · 2 hours ago

A strikingly pale 'ghost' lobster, the kind of find that only turns up about once in a hundred million catches, has a new home just in time for Halloween. Instead of ending up on a dinner plate, it was donated to the University of New England's Marine Science Center in Maine. Scientists there get the rare chance to study what makes its coloring so unusual, which could teach us more about lobster genetics. It's a happy ending for a one-of-a-kind creature, and a neat opportunity for researchers and visitors alike.
