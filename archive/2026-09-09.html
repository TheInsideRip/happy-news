Yard Work Reveals Largest Trove of Viking Silver Ever Discovered in Denmark

Good News Network · just now

A man in Denmark was just digging a new terrace in his backyard when his shovel hit something unexpected. What he found turned out to be the largest hoard of Viking-era silver ever unearthed in the country. Archaeologists are thrilled, since discoveries like this help fill in gaps about how people lived over a thousand years ago. It's a wonderful reminder that history is still hiding right under our feet, waiting to be found.
