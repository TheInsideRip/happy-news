Pittsburgh Water Launches Debt Forgiveness Program to Help Families with Rising Costs

Good News Network · 2 hours ago

Pittsburgh's water authority just rolled out a new program that wipes away old water bill debt for struggling residents over a 24-month period. Customers who qualify have already been enrolled as of September, and all they need to do is keep up with their regular monthly payments going forward. This takes a real weight off families who've been falling behind as utility costs climb. It's a practical, already-working fix, not just a promise on paper.
