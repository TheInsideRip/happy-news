New AI Model Can Detect Heart Disease Markers in Simple Electrocardiogram Readings

Good News Network · 4 hours ago

Scientists in the UK have built a new artificial intelligence tool that can spot early warning signs of heart disease from a simple, routine heart scan called an electrocardiogram. This is a scan many people already get, so doctors won't need any special new equipment to catch problems earlier. The AI can scan results in seconds and flag anything concerning for a doctor to review, acting like a extra set of careful eyes. Catching heart trouble sooner means more people can get treatment before a serious event happens. It's a simple upgrade to an already common test that could quietly save a lot of lives.

Korean Drone Rescue Program Helps Mozambique Achieve Zero Fatalities During Worst Flood in Decades

Good News Network · 4 hours ago

Back in January, a community in northern Mozambique faced one of the worst floods the region has seen in decades. Thanks to a squadron of drones flown by trained pilots, rescue teams could see exactly where people were trapped and get help to them fast. The result: not a single life was lost in the flooding. It's a powerful example of how a relatively small, smart piece of technology can turn a natural disaster from a tragedy into a story of successful teamwork.

Number of Young People Out of Work and Education Falls Below One Million in the UK

Positive News · 2 hours ago

New figures out of the UK show that the number of young people who are not in work, school, or training has dropped below one million for the first time in a while. That's real progress for a country that's had one of the highest rates of disconnected youth in Europe. It means more young adults are building skills, earning a paycheck, or working toward a future career instead of feeling stuck. Small shifts like this tend to ripple outward, helping families and communities too.
