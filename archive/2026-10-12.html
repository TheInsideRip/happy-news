‘Like Caring for a Pet’ – the App That Lets You Know if Your Neighborhood Tree Is Thirsty

Good News Network · 2 hours ago

In Berlin, a free app called Gieß den Kiez, which means 'water your neighborhood,' shows people a map of city trees near them and tells them which ones need watering. Thousands of residents have signed up to 'adopt' a tree, checking on it and giving it water just like a pet. It's helping the city's trees survive brutal summer heatwaves that have become more common. What started as a small city project has grown into a real community habit, with neighbors quietly keeping their streets green one watering can at a time.

Family Sells 650 Acres on East Texas Lake to Become State Park, Saying Land is 'Part of my soul'

Good News Network · 5 hours ago

A Texas family who has owned a beautiful stretch of land on Lake Palestine for generations has sold it so it can become a brand-new state park. The property is the longest unbroken piece of natural shoreline left on the lake, with pine woods, wetlands, bluffs, and pecan trees. Instead of selling to developers, the family chose to let everyone share the place they call part of their soul. Now hikers, campers, and nature lovers will get to enjoy this peaceful spot for generations to come. It's a lovely reminder that sometimes the most meaningful gift a family can give is simply sharing their favorite place with the world.
