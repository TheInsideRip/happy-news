Homeless Man Who Went Viral Playing Piano in a Church Has Now Raised $50,000 for Charity

Good News Network · just now

Two years ago, a homeless man named Rhys Wynne-Jones was filmed playing piano beautifully inside a church in Penzance, England, and the video went viral. Since then his life has completely turned around, and he's used his new platform to give back rather than just receive help. He has now raised an incredible $50,000 for charity through his music. It's a lovely reminder that talent and dignity don't disappear when someone loses their home, and that a moment of kindness can ripple outward for years. He went from sleeping rough to lifting others up.

Luxurious Bath Complex Used by Roman Emperor Found in Serbia

Good News Network · 22 hours ago

Archaeologists in Serbia have uncovered a stunning ancient Roman bath complex that once belonged to Emperor Maximinus Daza. The site includes three separate bathing pools and even a sauna, showing off some seriously impressive engineering for its time. It's a rare, well-preserved glimpse into daily luxury from the late Roman Empire. Finds like this help historians piece together how people actually lived nearly 1,700 years ago. It's a nice reminder that the past still has surprises buried just beneath our feet.
