'Autos for Love' Owners Delight in Giving Away Cars to Needy Neighbors

Good News Network · 8 hours ago

A couple who run an auto-repair shop in Texas used to refurbish one donated car a year and hand the keys to someone in their community who needed it. Thanks to their own generosity and gifts from others, they've been able to speed things up. Now multiple vehicles are going out to neighbors in need. For someone without reliable transportation, a car can mean getting to work, school, and the doctor.

Land Returned to Native American Ownership 400 Years After the First Thanksgiving

Good News Network · 5 hours ago

On October 30, The Nature Conservancy gave about 126 acres of salt marsh and natural habitat in Eastham, Massachusetts, to the Native Land Conservancy, an Indigenous-led land trust. The area is on Cape Cod, where the Pilgrims first explored more than 400 years ago. The land is now back in Native American hands, and the new owners will care for it. It's a quiet, hopeful way to mark Thanksgiving week.
