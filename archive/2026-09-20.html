Couple Fosters 59 Dogs in Retirement: 'We love to get them out of the kennels'

Good News Network · just now

A British couple in their 60s, Nicole and Gary Wordsworth, have spent the last 15 years opening their home to dogs in need. Working with the Battersea Dogs Home in Buckinghamshire, they've now fostered an incredible 59 pups, giving each one a warm bed and loving care while it waits for a forever family. Even in retirement, they have no plans to stop, saying they simply love getting the dogs out of the kennels and into a real home. It's a beautiful reminder of what steady, quiet kindness can add up to over the years. Every one of those 59 dogs got a softer landing because of them.

165-Mile Bike Trail Through Minnesota Finally Completed After 30 Years

Good News Network · just now

After three decades of planning, fundraising, and construction, Minnesota has finished a paved bike trail that stretches an incredible 165 miles. It winds from the Mississippi River all the way up to the wild Boundary Waters, linking small towns and natural scenery along the way. That makes it one of the longest paved cycling trails in the entire country. It's a huge win for outdoor lovers, local economies, and anyone who wants a beautiful, car-free way to get around. Decades of community persistence finally paid off in a big way.

New York City is Putting 'The Park' Back in Park Avenue – And Adding a Protected Bike Lane

Good News Network · 14 hours ago

New York City just unveiled a plan to bring back the leafy, walkable feel that Park Avenue had a century ago, before it became mostly a wide road for cars. The redesign will add more trees, benches, and green space, plus a protected bike lane running the length of the avenue. It's a hopeful sign that one of Manhattan's busiest streets could soon feel like an actual park again, living up to its name. The goal is a safer, greener stretch of the city for everyone who walks, bikes, or just wants a shady bench to sit on.
