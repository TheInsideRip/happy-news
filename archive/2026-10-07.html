Saudi Solar Park Proves Brilliant Breeding Ground for Threatened Sand Gazelles

Good News Network · 4 hours ago

A massive solar farm on Saudi Arabia's Red Sea coast has turned into an unexpected safe haven for endangered Arabian sand gazelles. Because the fenced, quiet site keeps out predators and poachers, the gazelles have been breeding steadily and their numbers are climbing. It's a lovely example of clean energy infrastructure doing double duty for wildlife. Conservationists say the herd is thriving better here than in many traditional reserves. One big solar project, two big wins for the planet.

The women putting community at the heart of elephant conservation

Positive News · 28 hours ago

Across parts of Africa, women are stepping into leadership roles in elephant conservation, bringing a community-first approach that's changing how wildlife protection works. Instead of top-down rules, these women are building trust with local villages, training neighbors as rangers and educators, and finding ways for people and elephants to share land peacefully. Their programs are already easing old tensions between farmers and herds and giving elephants safer corridors to roam. It's a quiet but real shift in who gets to lead conservation, and it's working.
