Nonprofit Brings Free Music Education to More Than 20,000 Miami Students in 131 Schools

Good News Network · 4 hours ago

A nonprofit in Miami-Dade County is now giving free music classes to about 21,000 students across 131 schools. For many kids, this is their only chance to learn an instrument or sing in a group, since school budgets often leave music out. Teachers say the program helps kids build confidence, make friends, and stay engaged in school. It's a lovely reminder of what communities can do when they decide every child deserves access to the arts.

Orphaned, Rescued, and Rewilded, Female Gorilla Becomes a Mother in Historic Conservation Success

Good News Network · just now

A female eastern lowland gorilla who was orphaned as a baby and later rescued and returned to the wild has just become a mother herself. She gave birth back in May in Virunga National Park in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, the very forest she was reintroduced to years ago. Eastern lowland gorillas are critically endangered, so every new baby matters, but this one is extra special because it proves rewilding orphaned gorillas can actually lead to them thriving and raising families of their own. It's a hopeful sign for the whole species' future.
